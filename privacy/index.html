THE ESSENTIALS

A straightforward approach.

This policy covers the public portfolio at timoclaessens.nl. Other applications or subdomains may have their own privacy practices.

Questions? Get in touch ↗
01

Who is responsible?

This personal website is maintained by Timo Claessens. For questions about this privacy policy or personal data, please email info@timoclaessens.nl.

02

What information is processed?

The portfolio does not provide user accounts or a contact form. When you visit the website, the hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address, browser details, requested pages and request times, to deliver the site and help protect and maintain the service.

If you email me, I will receive the details you choose to share and use them to respond to your message.

03

Cookies & analytics

The portfolio itself does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies, and no visitor-tracking scripts have been added to its code. Hosting infrastructure may use essential technical mechanisms; any future analytics or cookie changes should be reflected in this policy.

04

External websites

Some links lead to third-party websites, such as GitHub. Once you follow an external link, that service's own privacy policy applies. The portfolio does not automatically load third-party tracking scripts.

05

Retention & security

Technical logs may be retained by the hosting provider in accordance with its configuration and retention policies. Emails are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for correspondence and any applicable obligations. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

06

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data protection law, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to certain processing. Contact me using the email address below. You may also contact the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

07

Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The date at the top of the page indicates the latest published revision.
